Soft, then loud

Tap the table softly a few times. Then slap it hard.

Watch: the wave gets taller when the sound is louder. That height is called amplitude.

Low, then high

Hum a low note. Then hum a high one. Using the tone maker? Slide the pitch from left to right.

Watch: a high note squeezes the waves closer together. How often the wave repeats is called frequency.
