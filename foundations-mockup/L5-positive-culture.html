LESSON 5 · READ · 5 MIN

Building a Positive Culture

Girls do their best thinking when they feel they belong. These four habits do most of the work.

Start with movement

Open every session with a short dance warm-up. It sets the energy and tells girls this space is different.

Give everyone a role

Dance Crew Roles give each girl a job on her team, so every voice has a place.

Celebrate effort out loud

Name what you see: a brave question, a new move, a fixed bug. Pathway milestones make it visible.

Make agreements together

In the first session, let girls write the crew's agreements. They keep them better when they wrote them.
