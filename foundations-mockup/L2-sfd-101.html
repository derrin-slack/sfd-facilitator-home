STEM From Dance 101

Two minutes on who we are and why your Club matters.

OUR MISSION

STEM From Dance equips girls to become the innovators, engineers, and creators the world needs, building creativity, confidence, and skills through the transformative power of dance.

OUR VISION

A future where girls from every community lead in STEM careers, drive innovation, and build economic prosperity for their families and communities.

WHAT WE VALUE

Center Community

Small groups help each other through hard things.

Believe in Possibility

Every girl can see herself in STEM.

Strive for Excellence

Joyful and rigorous, every session.

Invest in Innovation

From AI to data science, girls build real skills.

Movement

Dance is how we learn, not a break from learning.

Stem From Trust

Girls, families and facilitators can count on us.
